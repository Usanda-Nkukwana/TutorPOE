Teaching practice

Tutorial delivery

I aim to create supportive, practical learning experiences that help students build confidence with technical concepts.

My approach

Make concepts clear

I break technical ideas into manageable steps and connect new concepts with practical examples.

Practise through doing

In programming, hands-on coding exercises help learners apply principles and explore how solutions work.

Build problem-solving

I encourage students to work through problems methodically and apply database or programming knowledge.

Support participation

A welcoming learning environment makes it easier for students to ask questions, test ideas, and learn from mistakes.

Facilitation

I used these techniques to encourage participation and check how well students understood the content.

Redirecting questions

When a student asked me a question, I invited other students to share their ideas before adding my own explanation. This encouraged interaction and helped me identify gaps in students’ understanding.

Allowing wait time

After asking a question, I gave students time to think before inviting them to respond. This gave them space to form and share their answers.

Checking for understanding

I asked students to explain concepts in their own words. Their explanations helped me assess their understanding and decide whether to continue or clarify the content further.

Practice informed by experience

My tutoring roles have included guiding Technical Programming (Java) students through programming principles, object-oriented concepts, and coding exercises, as well as helping Information Systems students with database concepts and their practical application.
