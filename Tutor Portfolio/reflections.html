Professional reflection

Reflections & lessons learnt

Tutoring has strengthened my appreciation for clear communication, practical learning, and continued professional growth.

Making technical ideas approachable

Programming and database concepts can feel abstract until learners have a chance to apply them. My tutoring experience has reinforced the value of pairing explanations with practical exercises and worked examples.

Learning is part of teaching

Helping students understand a topic encourages me to revisit the foundations, check that my explanations are clear, and consider more than one way to approach a problem. Continuing my own studies supports that commitment to learning.

What I want to keep developing

  • Continue improving how I explain complex ideas in simple, structured steps.
  • Keep connecting theory with practice and opportunities for independent problem-solving.
  • Gather feedback and reflect on it to improve future tutorials.
  • Document lesson resources and examples of learning, while respecting student privacy.
